About

Sri Lanka, island country lying in the Indian Ocean. It is located between latitudes 5°55′ and 9°51′ N and longitudes 79°41′ and 81°53′ E and has a maximum length of 268 miles (432 km) and a maximum width of 139 miles (224 km).

History

Proximity to the Indian subcontinent has facilitated close cultural interaction between Sri Lanka and India from ancient times.
At the crossroads of maritime routes traversing the Indian Ocean, Sri Lanka has also been exposed to cultural influences from other Asian civilizations.
It officially became Sri Lanka in 1972.

Civilization

Sri Lanka's civilization, dating back to the 6th century BCE, thrives on the preservation of Theravada Buddhism and an irrigation system. Its administrative divisions include nine provinces and 25 districts, reflecting its rich cultural influences.

Languages

Sri Lanka, a country known for its cultural diversity, officially recognizes three languages within its government and society:
  1. Sinhala
  2. English
  3. Tamil
